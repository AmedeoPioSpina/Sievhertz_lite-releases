## Features

- 🔎 **Search** songs, artists, albums and playlists, with infinite scrolling
- 🏠 **Home** with your YouTube Music recommendations, recently played and liked songs
- 🎤 **Artist pages** (top songs, albums, singles, similar artists) and **album / playlist pages**, Spotify style
- ❤️ **Your library**: favorite songs, your own playlists, saved albums and followed artists
- 📜 **Queue** you can reorder by dragging, shuffle, repeat, media keys
- 👤 Optional **YouTube account** connection (browser or `cookies.txt`) for your personal music
- 🌍 English and Italian
- 🔄 **Automatic, signed updates**
- 🪶 Tiny and fast: native Windows webview, no Electron

## Installing

1. Download the **setup.exe** and open it.
2. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**.
   This appears because the app has no paid Microsoft certificate. Every update is still **signed** and verified by the app before installing.
3. Open **Sievhertz Lite** from the Start menu.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. WebView2 is needed and is already part of Windows 10/11.

## Updates

The app checks for a new version a few seconds after starting and every 6 hours. When one is out you'll see a popup with what's new and an **Install and restart** button. You can also check manually in **Settings → About and updates**.
